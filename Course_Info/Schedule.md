# Course Schedule and Office Hours

The course schedule is maintained below. Note that this schedule is subject to change during the semester, so check back often!

Readings are from Dey & Wang, [*Computational Topology for Data Analysis*](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/tamaldey/book/CTDAbook/CTDAbook.pdf) (DW, free online) unless marked otherwise; EH is Edelsbrunner & Harer, *Computational Topology: An Introduction*. See the [Textbook page](Textbook.md) for details.
